But why ICT?

I choose Information and Communication Technology (ICT) because I am passionted about technology , mainly game development. This profession combines my love for art and creativity on one hand and on the other my interest in coding and how to make a whole new virtual world from scratch.

Another reason that lead me to my decision is the amount of carreer oportunities with ICT degree.
